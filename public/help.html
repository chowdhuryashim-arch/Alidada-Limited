2. Delegated financial limits

Every User and Super User has a financial limit delegated by a Super User. A new account starts at 0 — until a limit is assigned, every entry that person makes is routed for approval. Each change is recorded in the limit history and the person receives a message.

3. How a transaction flows

1 · Initiate. Click Add entry and choose Expense, Receive Fund or Transfer. Enter the amount, date (DD/MM/YYYY), description, category and account. As you type the amount, the form tells you what will happen.
2a · Within your limit → posted. If the amount is not more than your delegated limit, the entry is committed to the ledger immediately.
2b · Beyond your limit → sent for approval. The entry is passed to a user whose limit covers the amount (by default the one with the lowest sufficient limit; you may choose another). That approver receives a message: “Approval required”.
3 · Approve or reject. The approver opens Approvals → Awaiting my approval, reviews the entry and approves (optional remark) or rejects (reason required).
4 · Back to the initiator. On approval the entry returns to the person who initiated it, who receives a message: “Approved — ready for final posting”. On rejection they receive the reason.
5 · Final posting. The initiator opens Approvals → Ready for my final posting and clicks Final post. Only now is the entry part of the ledger, balances and statements.

Only posted entries count toward totals, balances, budgets and statements. Pending, approved-but-unposted, rejected and withdrawn entries are visible in Transactions and Approvals. Until your own entry is approved and posted, it also stays on your Dashboard under “My transactions awaiting approval”, showing whose approval it is waiting for, and it appears in Transactions marked Pending approval. Pending entries are never included in totals.

Other workflow actions

  • Withdraw — the initiator may withdraw an entry that is pending approval or approved but not yet posted.
  • Change approver — the initiator may re-route a pending entry to another eligible approver (for example if the approver is away or has been disabled; you receive a message when that happens).
  • Reverse — posted entries are never edited or deleted. To correct one, create a reversal: a new opposite entry dated today, which follows the same limit and approval rules.
  • Edit details — category, tags and note can be changed by the initiator or a Super User; every change is recorded in the entry’s approval trail.

4. Messages

The bell in the top bar shows unread messages. You receive a message when an entry is sent to you for approval, when your entry is approved and back with you for final posting, when it is rejected, when your limit changes, and for other workflow events. Messages marked Action needed clear automatically once you act. The app checks for new messages every 30 seconds.

Phone notifications

The same messages can pop up on your phone, like an app alert, even when the Ledger Book is closed. You get one when your approval is needed, when your entry is approved and back with you for final posting, when it is rejected, when you need to choose another approver, and when your financial limit changes.

  1. On your phone, open the Ledger Book and sign in.
  2. iPhone only: tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then open the Ledger Book from that new icon (needs iOS 16.4 or later).
  3. Go to Settings → Phone notifications and tap Turn on notifications for this device. Allow notifications when the phone asks.
  4. Tap Send a test notification to check it arrives.

Turn it on separately on each phone or computer you use. Signing out stops that device receiving your alerts; signing in again turns them back on. Tapping an alert opens your Messages. Alerts are free: they use the phone’s own browser notification service, not SMS.

For the Admin: every new user must have a mobile number. The Users page shows how many devices each person has turned on, a Send test notification button, and whether each alert was delivered. Phone notifications are switched on once per server by the Deployer with node scripts/setup-push.mjs (add --test for the Test Server).

5. Terms used

  • Description — what the entry is for (payee, customer, purpose).
  • Receive Fund — money coming into an account (a credit).
  • Expense — money paid out of an account (a debit).
  • Transfer — movement between two company accounts, e.g. bank → Petty Cash. Not counted as received or spent.
  • Loan given / Loan recovery — money lent to a person, and money received back from them. Not counted as received or spent; held in the Loans to persons account until recovered.
  • Voucher no. — the unique reference (ALD-000123) given to every entry.

6. Petty Cash

Petty Cash is a protected system account that cannot be removed. Its balance is always computed from posted entries and can never go negative: an expense or transfer out of Petty Cash larger than its balance is refused, both when it is entered and at final posting. Fund it with a Transfer from another account (Dashboard → “Fund Petty Cash”).

Loans to a person

Use Loans when the company pays money to a person (an employee advance, a personal loan) that will be paid back. A loan is not an expense and not a fund received: the money moves into the protected Loans to persons account and comes back out of it as it is recovered, so income, expenses and budgets are never affected.

  1. Give a loan. Open Loans → Give a loan. Enter the borrower’s name (and mobile, optional), the amount, the date, the account it is paid from, and a purpose if you like. Each loan gets a number such as LN-0001.
  2. Record a recovery. When the person pays back, open the loan and click Record recovery, or use Loans → Record recovery (also reachable from Add entry). Choose the borrower from the list, which shows only people who were given a loan and still owe money, with their loan number and outstanding amount. No new name can be entered for a recovery. Enter the amount received, the date and the account it was received into. You can recover in full or in parts on different dates; Recover in full fills in the whole outstanding amount. Excess recovery is not allowed: if the amount is more than the person owes, the form says so and cannot be saved.
  3. Outstanding. The Loans page shows, for every loan, what was paid out, what has been recovered and what is still outstanding. A loan closes as Fully recovered when nothing is left.
  4. Pay more. To lend the same person more on the same loan, open it and click Pay more. For a separate loan, use Give a loan again.
  5. Loan status report (on the Loans page) lists each person with every loan, the amount paid out, recovered and still outstanding, and the date of the last recovery, as on any date you choose. Pick one person or all, and outstanding loans only or all loans, then print or save as PDF.
  6. Print loan statement from the loan gives a statement for that person with every payment and recovery, the running outstanding balance and a space for the borrower’s signature.

Loan payments and recoveries follow the same financial-limit rules as every other entry: within your limit they post immediately, beyond it they go for approval and come back to you for final posting. Only posted entries change the outstanding amount. A recovery can never be more than is outstanding (recoveries already entered but not yet posted count too), and a loan paid from Petty Cash can never overdraw it. A mistake is corrected with Reverse on the entry, as usual.

7. Duplicate caution

If a new entry has the same date, description, amount, account and type as an existing one, the form shows the match and changes the button to Add anyway. Nothing is blocked — you decide.

8. Printing a ledger statement

Click Print in the top bar, choose an account (or all), optionally a tag to report only entries with that tag, and a date range (pick a preset such as “This month”, or set any From and To dates). The Transactions page has the same Tag filter and a “Custom range…” period, and its CSV download follows the filters. The statement opens in a new tab with the company name, total credit, total debit, net and Petty Cash balance boxes (plus opening and closing balances for a single account), a Debit/Credit detail table, a category summary, and signature lines. Allow pop-ups for this site if nothing opens.

9. For the Admin

  1. Open Users → New user, choose User, Mid User or Super User, enter the mobile number, enter name, designation, username and a temporary password.
  2. Share the username and temporary password privately. The person must set their own password at first sign-in.
  3. Create at least two Super Users so that they can assign each other’s limits.
  4. Disabling a user signs them out immediately and asks anyone waiting on their approval to choose another approver.

10. Backup

Super Users can download a JSON backup from Settings at any time and restore it later (a typed confirmation is required). Backups contain ledger data only — never users or passwords.
